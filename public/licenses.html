About CADDY

CADDY is a parametric 3D CAD program for designing parts for 3D printing. It runs entirely in your browser: there are no accounts and nothing is uploaded. Your projects are stored on your own device.

Geometry engine: OpenCascade (LGPL 2.1)

CADDY's solid modelling is done by OpenCascade Technology (OCCT), compiled to WebAssembly (the replicad-opencascadejs build of opencascade.js). OpenCascade is free software under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1.

  • OpenCascade source code: github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT
  • Build used here (source and build files): replicad-opencascadejs
  • The engine is a separate file (assets/*.wasm) loaded by the page, so it can be replaced with your own build of the same library.

Other components

  • replicad (geometry library): MIT license
  • three.js (3D display): MIT license
